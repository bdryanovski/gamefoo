Camera Issues Found

  • Engine.render() never applies ctx.translate() — camera tracks position but doesn't transform the canvas
  • Camera is private on the engine (this.engine.camera) — entities can't access it for manual transforms
  • No world-to-screen coordinate conversion — all entities render at world coords regardless of camera
  • All objects are updated/rendered every frame even when off-screen — no frustum culling
  • Background fill uses canvas size, not world size — moving camera would reveal gaps
  • This demo uses a manual workaround: a shared camera offset that entities subtract in their render() methods
